# Playtest Campaign: Heartlands Shadows (Levels 1–30)

An epic-scale campaign playtest framework set in the **Forgotten Realms Heartlands**, engineered to systematically stress-test and calibrate the **RoL d20** ruleset across every tier of play from apprentice to demigod.

---

## 1. Campaign Concept & Setting

### The Heartlands Canvas
* **Primary Geography**: The borderlands between **Cormyr**, **Sembia**, the **Dalelands**, and the **Moonsea**, framed by the **Storm Horns**, the **Thunder Peaks**, the **Hullack Forest**, and the desert wastes of southern **Anauroch**.
* **Central Faction War**: A multi-layered cold war turning hot:
  * **The Zhentarim** (The Black Network): Operating out of Darkhold, Zhentil Keep, and secret trade outposts, pushing shadow caravans and mercenary contracts.
  * **The Harpers**: Covert operatives, bards, and rangers working to prevent tyrannical consolidation and protect ancient elven wards.
  * **The Crown of Cormyr & The War Wizards**: Maintaining royal law, monster borders, and arcane licenses against subversive merchant cabals.
  * **Sembian Merchant Princes & The Night Masks / Fire Knives**: Oligarchs manipulating commodity prices, smuggling illicit Netherese relics, and funding private assassin guilds.
  * **The Red Wizards of Thay**: Operating legitimate enclave trade fronts while secretly researching planar breaches and necrotic rituals.
  * **The Cult of the Dragon & Shadowfell Incursions**: Seeking dracoliches and awakening slumbering beasts beneath the Thunder Peaks.

### Pacing & Accelerated Progression
* **Progression Scope**: **Level 1 to Level 30** (Full Epic Tier).
* **Accelerated Experience Curve**: XP awards set to **150%–200% of standard baseline** (or structured milestone chapters) to ensure all tiers are thoroughly playtested without stalling in real-world time.

## 2. Campaign Arc & Five-Tier Structure

```
TIER 1 (Lvl 1–4)   BORDERLAND SHADOWS
                   • Local threats around Arabel, Wheloon, and the Hullack Forest
                   • Banditry, goblinoid raids, smuggling tunnels, first taste of insidious poison

TIER 2 (Lvl 5–10)  THE MERCHANTS & THE BLACK NETWORK
                   • Regional intrigue across Sembian cities (Selgaunt, Daerlun) and Thunder Peaks
                   • High-stakes urban infiltration, chase scenes, mounted skirmishes, sea piracy

TIER 3 (Lvl 11–16) THE REAWAKENING MYTHALS
                   • Ancient elven ruins of Myth Drannor, Underdark incursions beneath the Moonsea
                   • Mass combat / siege warfare, anti-magic / wild magic zones, underwater vaults

TIER 4 (Lvl 17–20) WAR FOR THE HEARTLANDS
                   • Full-scale planar breaches, Netherese floating citadel remnants, archmages
                   • Planar journeys to the Ethereal, Astral, and Elemental Planes; high-tier item crafting

TIER 5 (Lvl 21–30) EPIC TRANSCENDENCE & GODS' SHADOWS
                   • Cosmic threats, Avatars of Bane, Cyric, and Shar; epic demigod-tier powers
                   • Direct planar domain warfare, reality-shaping spells, mythic prestige skills
```

| Feature / Subsystem | Targeted Playtest Focus | Primary Testing Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Full Level 1–30 Span** | Mathematical stability of attack vs. defense formulas, HP/SP scaling, and PP pools up to epic 30. | Tiers 1 through 5 |
| **Broad Class Coverage** | Testing martial warriors, divine conduits, arcane manipulators, psions, and hybrid skirmishers. | All Tiers |

| **Social Standing & Economy** | Tracking **Wealth Category (WC)**, **Social Category (SC)**, and equipment availability across lowborn mercenaries up to Cormyrian nobility. | Tiers 1–4 |

## 4. Tier-by-Tier Adventure Outline

### Tier 1: The Borderland Shadows (Levels 1–4)
* **Setting**: The border between Cormyr and the Dalelands (Eveningstar, Arabel, Hullack Forest).
* **Key Adventures**:
  1. *The Broken Wagon at King's Forest*: Introduction to combat, darkness, surprise, and tracking.
  2. *The Rat-Catchers of Arabel*: Urban sewers, disease (filth fever), brawling, and light vs. heavy weapons.
  3. *The Smuggler's Cove on the Wyvernwater*: First small vehicle/boat combat, water hazards, traps, and poison.
  4. *Assault on Wolf Tooth Keep*: Goblinoid and Zhentarim outpost assault, testing reach weapons and defensive armor DR.

### Tier 2: The Merchants & The Black Network (Levels 5–10)
* **Setting**: Sembia (Selgaunt, Daerlun) and the Thunder Peaks passes.
* **Key Adventures**:
  1. *Rooftops of Selgaunt*: High-speed parkour chase scene, stealth, vital attacks, and urban social influence.
  2. *Corsairs of the Dragonmere*: Naval combat, boarding maneuvers, ballista fire, swimming, and water hazards.
  3. *The Thunder Peaks Caravan Escort*: Mounted combat, extreme mountain blizzard weather, cliffside ambushes.
  4. *The Infiltration of Darkhold Outpost*: Mid-tier dungeon crawl, poison traps, prestige skills, and multi-attack balance.

### Tier 3: The Reawakening Mythals (Levels 11–16)
* **Setting**: Myth Drannor, Lake Sember, and the Moonsea depths.
* **Key Adventures**:
  1. *The Sunken Halls of Lake Sember*: Deep underwater combat, pressure/drowning rules, aquatic monster grapples.
  2. *The Siege of Tilver's Gap*: Full mass combat and siege engine warfare against a Zhentarim horde; artillery vs. fortifications.
  3. *Ruins of Myth Drannor*: Wild magic zones, dead-magic zones, ancient elven constructs, arcane vs. divine resource drain.
  4. *Descent into the Moonsea Underdark*: Aberrations, sensory deprivation, insanity/madness mechanics, and insidious damage.

### Tier 4: War for the Heartlands (Levels 17–20)
* **Setting**: Cormyrian planar gates, Netherese flying ruins, the High Astral.
* **Key Adventures**:
  1. *The Sky-Dreadnought of Netheril*: Aerial combat, vehicle maneuvering at 10,000 feet, fall damage mechanics, high-tier magic item usage.
  2. *The Astral Conduit*: Planar navigation, subjective gravity, psychic combat, psionic vs. arcane duels.
  3. *The Citadel of the Red Wizards*: Mass planar conjurations, high-tier battlefield control spells vs. antimagic counter-measures.

### Tier 5: Epic Transcendence (Levels 21–30)
* **Setting**: Outer Planes, the Throne of Bane, and Faerûn-spanning cataclysms.
* **Key Adventures**:
  1. *The Shadow over the Heartlands*: Avatars of the Dead Three invading the mortal plane; reality-warping epic spells.
  2. *The Elemental Crucible*: Venturing into the Elemental Planes to harvest primal energy to reforge ancient mythals.
  3. *Ascension & Legacy*: Demigod-tier prestige skills, planar fortress management, and the ultimate test of high-level RoL d20 mathematical stability.
